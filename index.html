🌌 Local Host Versus Mode

🛸 FARM SPACE: MANUAL 🚀

🎮 Controls

  • 1 - 5 Select Hotbar Inventory Slots
  • Arrows Move your Farmer
  • A Swing Scythe (Plow ground)
  • Spacebar Plant Watermelon Seed (Must stand on Plowed Dirt)
  • S Fire Equipped AK-47 Weapon
  • D Interact: Grab/Drop Animals & Pick up/Throw Grenades
  • P Pause / Unpause Game

🌾 Core Game Loop

  1. Plow: Equip your Scythe and press [A] to make rich brown soil patches.
  2. Plant: Walk over the patch and press [Spacebar] to plant a crop.
  3. Harvest & Defend: When the watermelon is fully grown, walk over it to harvest it. It will automatically shoot down the nearest alien threat!
  4. Rescue Animals: Pick up Space Pigs/Chickens with [D] and bring them to the corral. They spawn floating Charms (+5 Score) as a reward!

📦 Supply Drops & Tractors

Score kills to trigger crates. Grab Tractor Wheels to gain speed and temporary invincibility, running over aliens instantly for 10 seconds!
