🎮 Controls

  • 1 - 5 Select Hotbar Inventory Slots
  • Arrows Move your Farmer
  • A Swing Scythe (Plow ground)
  • Spacebar Plant Watermelon Seed
  • S Fire Equipped Weapon
  • D Interact: Grab/Drop Animals & Pick up/Throw Grenades
  • P Pause / Unpause Game

🌾 Core Game Loop

  1. Plow: Equip your Scythe and press [A] to make rich brown soil patches.
  2. Plant: Walk over the patch and press [Spacebar] to plant a crop.
  3. Harvest: Harvest fully grown watermelons to shoot down aliens.
  4. Rescue: Pick up Space Pigs/Chickens with [D].
